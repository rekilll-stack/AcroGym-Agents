## What rivals over-do
- A free trial as the main hook
- Seasons and events that are a year out of date
- Borrowed school halls, including the camp venue at The Pearl
- Girls-only or play-only framing
- One-off camp weeks instead of an ongoing programme

## Format mix & cadence
- **Mix:** Reels 50%, carousels 40%, static ≤10%. Each week: at least 3 Reels and 2 carousels.
- **Carousels:** start with a hook slide that people will want to save, such as a checklist or an age guide. Mood-only carousels underperform.
- **Stories:** 3–4 a day, with polls, question stickers and a "which group fits?" quiz.
- **Timing:** 19:00. Weekends are strongest; avoid Friday.
- **Measuring Reels:** judge each Reel by **shares per reach**, not reach alone.
- **Testing:** test humour first as trial Reels shown to non-followers.
- **Call to action:** use exactly this line every time: **"First class 100 QAR, credited to your package."** Never use "link in bio" alone.
- **Housekeeping:**
  - Use searchable keywords (gymnastics Doha, kids acrobatics, mid-term activities, The Pearl, Lagoona Mall).
  - At most 5 hashtags.
  - Geotag Lagoona Mall.
  - Reply to DMs within 30 minutes.

## Voice & visual guardrails
- Warm, energetic, safe, professional. English first, Arabic where possible. Brand navy/orange.
- Ages are always "2 to 16" plus adults 18+.
- **There is no free trial.** Never write "trial" without "100 QAR, credited to your package".
- Never invent prices, dates, camps, names or testimonials. Never name rivals or quote their prices in content.
- A fresh caption every time; no reposts.
- Children from the 61 families who refused consent must never appear.
- The test: "Would a parent forward this?"
